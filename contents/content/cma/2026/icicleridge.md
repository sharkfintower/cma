I had a few days in the Leavenworth area, and right away
decided to do an old favorite hike: Icicle Ridge from town
all the way to the Fourth of July Creek Trail, which I'd
follow down into Icicle Canyon and then hitch a ride
back to my car. I'd done this alone or with friends
several times in the old days.

A neat thing happened on the hitchhike back to my car.
A few cars drove by, but then one stopped for me, driven
by a woman with her parents. I discovered the woman lives
in Germany, north of Munich, working for the American
military. So we had a nice conversation in German and the
parents were flabbergasted in an entertaining way.
Something about picking up a hitchhiker and discovering
this interesting connection just bowled them over.

So it was a very fun experience!

**15 miles, 6050 feet elevation gain.*
