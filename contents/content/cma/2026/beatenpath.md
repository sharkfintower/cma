*Most of these excellent photos are from Theron*

Theron was coming to town! Robert suggested the Beaten Path as
a fun backpacking trip for the three of us to do. We had good weather,
and would be able to arrange a car shuttle with two vehicles if I
drove up from Idaho Falls through Yellowstone to meet them at Cooke
City. No problemo!

In fact, we were really lucky, because Case was going to do the trip
as well. If we travelled north to south, then on exit, I could hop in
my Subaru, and Theron and Robert could take Case's car back to Bozeman,
and he would take Roberts truck. Amazing. Couldn't have planned it any
better.

We repaired to a camp spot, built a fire and enjoyed some burgers in
the sunset.

In search of a great campsite, we followed a trail all the
way to Echo Lake, which we had to ourselves. Good food, a
fantastic warm fire, and hours of joking around and
philosophy made for a great night.

## Day Two, 8 miles

I took off a bit early, then sat to meditate and have a snack
above a cliff where the fellas caught me. The way the trail was
blasted into cliffs usually on the north side of the many lakes
was fantastic. Pretty gentle but steady elevation gain to the
country above timberline...

No fire tonight, but lots of fun listening to music and planning
future trips. We harkened back to a time that we never knew, but
somehow hope to recreate -- America before 1915: big ideas, confidence
in the pride and spirit of the nation, a vision of man and nature
in communion.

## Day Three, 10.5 miles

Again I took off early. The fellas were spoofed by an etheral voice
making whooping noises and thought it was me, playing the trickster.
No! It was a strange lady making sing-song noises above a cliff.
But they found me a 1/2 mile later on the edge of a cliff.

On the way out, I followed somewhat tiredly behind, perhaps unwilling
to say goodbye to the restful high country.

expected cold, snow and wind. It'll be here soon though!

Thanks Rob and Theron!
