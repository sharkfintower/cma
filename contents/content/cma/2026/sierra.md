Arjun had recommended a hike up White Mountain, one of the
high peaks of California, near the famous bristlecone pine
trees in the White Mountains.

I enjoyed this hike very much. It was a long drive to the
trailhead. I was worried I'd bake in the heat but it is very
high, and this didn't happen. Here are some pics:
